# Cedar & Oak Realty — simulated internal policy v1 (not legal advice)
[POL-01] Internal listing agents and coordinators may read assigned cases and create drafts only. Broker approves specific draft versions; no external send/publish in the MVP.
[POL-02] Inspection findings must cite source ID and distinguish observations, uncertainty and professional follow-up. No legal repair obligations or invented estimates.
[POL-03] Marketing drafts use verified attributes only. Flag unverified claims separately. Broker reviews accuracy and fair-housing concerns before release outside this MVP.
[POL-04] Market analysis requires authorized, dated, relevant comparable data. Default comps are unavailable for pricing; report data gaps. Do not infer a recommended list price.
[POL-05] Compare inventory to the brokerage-approved checklist; missing does not establish a legal obligation. HOA applicability unknown must be confirmed.
[POL-06] Backend verifies employee identity, case membership and tool permissions on every call; seller is a data subject, not a system user.
[POL-07] Store source IDs, uncertainty, reviewer, draft version, policy version, and status. A changed draft or source version requires re-review. Never approve on behalf of a broker.
